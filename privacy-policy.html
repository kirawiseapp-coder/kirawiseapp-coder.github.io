KiraWise Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 August 2026

KiraWise does not collect your data. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

This version of the app ships without the Android internet permission, so it has no ability to send anything anywhere. Should a later version add an optional account for backing up your data, this policy will be updated and published before that version is released.

What KiraWise stores

Everything you type into KiraWise is written to a database file inside the app's own private storage on your device:

  • transactions, categories, and notes
  • income sources and fixed monthly expenses
  • savings goals and progress
  • accounts you create, including their names, colours and icons
  • bill splits and the items in them
  • your settings, such as theme and hourly rate

Android keeps this storage private to the app. Other apps cannot read it.

Photos

If you choose a photo for an account, KiraWise opens the Android photo picker. You pick one image; the system hands only that image to the app. KiraWise never receives access to the rest of your photo library, which is why it does not ask for a photos permission.

The image you pick is cropped, resized to 512×512 pixels, and saved inside the app's private storage. It is not uploaded anywhere.

Backups you make yourself

Settings → Backup & restore writes a copy of your data to a file and hands it to Android's share sheet, where you choose what happens to it next.

KiraWise does not send that file anywhere. It has no internet permission and no ability to. Once you pick an app in the share sheet, the file is in that app's hands and covered by that app's privacy policy, not this one — so if you send a backup to a cloud drive or a chat app, please treat it as you would any other document containing your finances.

Restoring reads a file you pick and replaces what is currently in the app with its contents.

What KiraWise does not do

  • No account, no sign-up, no login
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or usage tracking
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No location access
  • No contacts, calendar, microphone, or camera access
  • No selling or sharing of data, because no data is collected

Third-party components

KiraWise is built with open-source software libraries that run entirely on your device: a local database engine, a chart drawing library, date and number formatting, and the system photo picker. None of them contact a server and none of them collect information about you.

Children

KiraWise is a general-audience budgeting tool. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Because your data exists only on your device, you control it completely:

  • Delete individual records inside the app at any time.
  • Delete everything by going to Android Settings → Apps → KiraWise → Storage → Clear data, or simply by uninstalling KiraWise.

Uninstalling removes the database and any account photos with it. There is no server-side copy for us to delete, because there is no server.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of KiraWise adds a feature that sends data off your device — for example optional cloud backup — this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the app will ask for your consent before any data leaves your phone.
